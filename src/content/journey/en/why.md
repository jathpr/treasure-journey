Happiness is a state of mind. The aim of the spiritual path is to learn to find
happiness and share it. Happiness lives in the mind, not outside it, so it is
important to learn how to navigate the mind.

[Practice](../practice/) helps us gain first-hand experience of this orientation.
[Theory](../theory/) gives us a general picture of how the mind is structured.
